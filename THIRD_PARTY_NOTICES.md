# Third-party materials

Competition problems, original solutions, PDFs and source figures belong to their respective authors, competition organizers and rights holders. Attribution and public URLs are retained in sources/manifest.json and in the original PDFs.

The source collection contains university-hosted reprints, third-party downloads and community-hosted historical papers. Materials include East China Normal University resources, cmathc.org.cn downloads and the public IceFerryLing/Math_competition collection. Community hosting and matching file hashes do not establish official CMC certification.

Public download availability does not establish an open redistribution license. No open license for the original competition materials was verified. This repository does not grant permission on behalf of third-party rights holders; substantiated correction or removal requests should be honored.

The project's mathematical restatements, solution summaries, derivations and rubrics are distinguished from original reference solutions. They are not presented as an official CMC release. No separate open data license is granted pending provenance and rights review.

Only the original software under tools/ and tests/ is covered by the MIT license. That software license does not cover third-party mathematical materials.
